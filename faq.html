Frequently Asked Questions

Clear answers to common questions about HS.

HS is not

  • Contagious.
  • A sexually transmitted infection.
  • An infection itself.
  • Caused by poor hygiene.

Your questions, answered

Is HS contagious?

No. HS cannot be passed from one person to another through touching lesions, sharing towels, or contact with drainage. New lesions can develop in other areas, but that is not HS being transmitted across the body.

Is HS a sexually transmitted infection (STI)?

No. Although HS can affect the genital area and buttocks, it is not an STI. You did not catch HS from someone, and you cannot give it to someone else.

Is HS an infection?

HS is an inflammatory condition, not an infection. Bacteria can be present in lesions, and secondary infections can occur. A healthcare professional can assess symptoms that may indicate infection.

Is HS caused by poor hygiene?

No. HS is not caused by how well you wash or by the products you use to wash your body or clothing.

Is HS an autoimmune disease?

HS is generally described as an autoinflammatory condition rather than a classic autoimmune disease. An overactive inflammatory response is involved; it is not typically understood as the same targeted attack on healthy tissue seen in classic autoimmune diseases.
