Your Journey. Your Goals. Your Next Step.

Beginning January 11, 2027 · Seven weeks · Virtual (online)

Registration

Newly diagnosed with HS? You're not alone.

Receiving a diagnosis of hidradenitis suppurativa (HS) can bring a mixture of emotions: relief at finally having answers, uncertainty about what lies ahead, and questions about how to manage life with a chronic condition.

You don't have to figure everything out at once.

HS Navigate is a seven-week, non-clinical coaching experience developed by the International Association of Hidradenitis Suppurativa Network (IAHSN) to help adults newly diagnosed with HS gain knowledge, build confidence, develop practical strategies, and take meaningful steps forward.

Through guided coaching, personal reflection, educational resources, and goal-setting activities, participants are encouraged to become more informed, empowered, and confident in navigating their HS journey.

Your diagnosis is part of your story. It doesn't define your entire journey.

What is HS Navigate?

HS Navigate is a structured, seven-week virtual coaching experience designed specifically for adults who have recently been diagnosed with hidradenitis suppurativa.

The program combines education, practical tools, guided reflection, and personalized goal-setting to help participants better understand their condition and navigate the challenges that may arise after diagnosis.

Rather than focusing solely on the medical aspects of HS, HS Navigate recognizes that living with a chronic condition can affect many areas of life, including emotional wellness, relationships, daily routines, and self-confidence.

The goal is to help participants develop the knowledge, skills, and confidence to become active participants in their own HS journey.

Who is HS Navigate for?

HS Navigate is designed for individuals who:

  • Are 18 years of age or older.
  • Reside in the United States.
  • Have recently received a diagnosis of hidradenitis suppurativa.
  • Want to better understand HS and available treatment approaches.
  • Would benefit from guidance in navigating daily life with HS.
  • Are interested in developing personal goals and practical coping strategies.
  • Are ready to participate in a structured coaching experience.

No one begins their HS journey with all the answers. HS Navigate provides a place to begin learning, exploring, and moving forward.

What will you gain from HS Navigate?

Learn: Understand Your HS

Build a foundation of knowledge about hidradenitis suppurativa, including common symptoms, treatment approaches, and the importance of ongoing medical care.

Build: Develop Practical Strategies

Explore tools and strategies for managing daily routines, communicating needs, preparing for healthcare appointments, and navigating challenges associated with living with HS.

Gain Confidence: Take an Active Role

Identify personal strengths, establish realistic goals, practice self-advocacy, and develop greater confidence in making informed decisions about your well-being.

Connect: Discover Support and Resources

Learn how to identify helpful resources, strengthen your support network, and recognize that you do not have to navigate life with HS alone.

Your seven-week HS Navigate experience

Each week builds upon the previous one, creating opportunities for learning, personal reflection, and practical application.

  1. Week 1 Understanding Your HS Diagnosis
  2. Week 2 Navigating Healthcare and Treatment Conversations
  3. Week 3 Emotional Wellness and Self-Awareness
  4. Week 4 Daily Living and Practical Strategies
  5. Week 5 Communication, Relationships, and Self-Advocacy
  6. Week 6 Developing Your Personal HS Navigation Plan
  7. Week 7 Reflection, Growth, and Next Steps

Weekly topics are a proposed overview and will be aligned with the final HS Navigate curriculum.

What will participants receive?

HS Navigate provides resources designed to support learning and personal growth throughout the seven-week experience.

HS Navigate Participant Workbook

A guided workbook containing educational information, reflection exercises, personal activities, and goal-setting tools.

Guided Coaching Sessions

Six structured coaching sessions facilitated by trained HS Navigate coaches to encourage reflection, learning, practical problem-solving, and personal development.

Papaya

A structured coaching resource incorporated into the first three weeks of the program to support learning and reflection.

HS Journey Companion

A resource designed to help participants document experiences, explore personal goals, and continue applying what they learn.

Week Seven Reflection

An opportunity to reflect on personal growth, recognize progress, and identify next steps for continuing the HS journey.

Continued Follow-Up

Participants will have scheduled follow-up opportunities at three, six, and twelve months after completing the program to reflect on progress and continued goals.

Program information

  • Program begins: January 11, 2027.
  • Duration: Seven weeks.
  • Format: Virtual (online).
  • Program structure: Six guided coaching sessions followed by a seventh week of reflection.
  • Who it is for: Adults newly diagnosed with hidradenitis suppurativa.
  • Offered by: International Association of Hidradenitis Suppurativa Network (IAHSN).

HS Navigate is coaching, not medical treatment

HS Navigate is a non-clinical coaching and educational experience. The program does not provide medical diagnoses, medical treatment, psychotherapy, or individualized medical advice.

HS Navigate coaches help participants explore goals, develop practical strategies, identify resources, and build confidence in navigating life with HS.

Participants should continue working with their dermatologists and other healthcare professionals for medical evaluation, treatment, and ongoing clinical care.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need to be newly diagnosed with HS to participate?

Yes. HS Navigate is specifically designed for adults who are newly diagnosed with hidradenitis suppurativa and beginning to navigate their diagnosis.

Is HS Navigate a support group?

No. HS Navigate is a structured, non-clinical coaching experience focused on education, personal reflection, goal-setting, and practical strategies.

Will HS Navigate help me choose medical treatments?

The program provides general education about HS and encourages informed conversations with healthcare professionals. Coaches do not recommend or prescribe medical treatments.

Will I receive materials?

Yes. Participants will receive access to the HS Navigate Participant Workbook and other program resources.

What happens after the seven-week program?

Participants will have follow-up opportunities at three, six, and twelve months to reflect on their progress and continued goals.

Can I participate from anywhere in the United States?

Yes. HS Navigate is offered virtually to eligible adults residing in the United States.

For healthcare professionals

Help your newly diagnosed patients take their next step.

An HS diagnosis can leave patients with questions that extend beyond medical treatment. HS Navigate offers a structured, non-clinical coaching experience designed to complement, not replace, ongoing medical care.

Through education, personal reflection, practical tools, and goal-setting, the program helps newly diagnosed adults build confidence in navigating life with hidradenitis suppurativa.

Healthcare professionals are encouraged to share information about HS Navigate with eligible patients who may benefit from additional educational and coaching support.

Take the next step

HS Navigate offers an opportunity to gain knowledge, discover resources, build confidence, and begin moving forward, one step at a time.
