## Medical wording for Donna to review

The supplied material was retained in meaning, with limited accuracy/clarity changes on What Is HS and the general FAQ:

- Follicular blockage and inflammation are described without claiming bacteria are the established cause of HS.
- The progression paragraph says progression to severe disease is not inevitable, rather than saying there is no evidence that HS can progress.
- HS itself is not an infection; the FAQ acknowledges that secondary infections can occur.
- Risk-factor wording uses current NIAMS estimates and avoids treating risk factors as a diagnosis.
- Autoinflammatory vs. autoimmune wording avoids an absolute claim that inflammation never affects healthy tissue.

Sources appear on the educational pages: NIAMS, the American Academy of Dermatology, and DermNet. These pages are educational and should receive IAHSN's medical-content approval before publication.
