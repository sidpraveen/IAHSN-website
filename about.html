Our Mission

IAHSN provides whole-person, patient-centered support through advocacy, education, emotional wellbeing, community engagement, and empowering programs.

No one should have to navigate HS without trusted information and compassionate support.

Be part of the IAHSN community and stay informed with the latest news, trusted education, upcoming events, and opportunities to connect.

  • Early access to workshops, webinars, and special events
  • Updates on IAHSN programs and initiatives
  • Inspiring community stories and advocacy news
  • Announcements about new resources and ways to get involved
